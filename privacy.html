Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Learn how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you visit or shop with PROEG².

01

Information We Collect

When you visit our site, we automatically collect certain information about your device, including information about your web browser, IP address, time zone, and some of the cookies that are installed on your device. Additionally, as you browse, we collect information about the individual web pages or products you view.

02

How We Use Your Personal Information

We use the Order Information that we collect generally to fulfill any orders placed through the Site (including processing your payment information, arranging for shipping, and providing you with invoices and/or order confirmations).

03

Sharing Your Information

We share your Personal Information with third parties to help us use your Personal Information, as described above. We also use analytics tools to help us understand how our customers interact with the Site.

04

Data Security & Retention

We maintain bank-grade 256-bit encryption standards across all payment and checkout pipelines operated under SIA APF Trading in Mārupe, Latvia. When you place an order through the site, we maintain your Order Information for our records unless and until you request its deletion.
