Understanding Bioavailability & Net Protein Utilization

Biological Value (BV) measures how efficiently protein is incorporated into human tissue. Egg protein historically holds a BV score of 100 as the gold standard. With 99% net protein utilization, virtually every gram consumed reaches systemic circulation without metabolic waste.

Ovomet® vs. Ovoderm®: Targeted Bio-Actives

Ovomet® is specifically formulated for joint mobility, tendon recovery, and cartilage cushioning. In contrast, Ovoderm® targets dermal fibroblast proliferation, boosting skin hydration, elasticity, and keratin production for hair and nail vitality.
